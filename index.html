Privacy policy

Application information

Weekly dispatch workbooks for ReliaRide staff.

ReliaRide X's creates weekly Google Sheets dispatch workbooks from an approved master template and maintains the driver, vehicle, and trip information used by authorized ReliaRide personnel.

The operational application and its workbooks are restricted to authorized staff.

What the application does

  • Copies the configured master into the designated Google Drive folder and prepares dated dispatch sheets.
  • Inherits the approved template's layout and native spreadsheet formatting.
  • Maintains dispatch information from configured operational sources and keeps private processing and audit records.

Why Google access is requested

An authorized account grants Google Drive and Google Sheets access so the application can find the configured template, create weekly workbooks, and maintain operational spreadsheets. Account identity identifies the authorizing user. Google Apps Script and Google Cloud permissions support scheduled execution and private processing records.

Read our privacy policy for how the application accesses, uses, stores, and shares Google user data.

Support and access

ReliaRide X's is a staff operations tool. For access, help with workbook creation, or privacy questions, contact scenicviewtransportation@gmail.com.
