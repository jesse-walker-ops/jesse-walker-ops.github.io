Privacy policy

Effective November 17, 2026

This policy describes how ReliaRide uses information through ReliaRide X's, an application for creating and maintaining private operational dispatch workbooks for authorized ReliaRide personnel.

Information the application accesses

With the authorizing user's permission, the application uses Google account identity, including the account's email address, and accesses configured Google Drive folders, templates, and Google Sheets workbooks. Operational records may contain staff names and availability, vehicle assignments, passenger names and contact information, trip locations and times, and dispatch notes.

How information is used

Google account identity is used to authorize access. Drive and Sheets data is used to locate approved templates, create and maintain weekly workbooks, update dispatch information, and verify results. Scheduled Google Apps Script execution and Google Cloud services support these functions. Private processing state and audit records help prevent duplicate processing and support recovery and authorized troubleshooting.

Storage and access

Operational workbooks are stored in configured Google Drive locations. Processing state and audit records are stored in private Google Cloud services. Access is controlled through authorized Google accounts, file permissions, and application access controls. The public information site does not provide access to operational workbooks or records.

Sharing and limits on use

Information is available to authorized ReliaRide personnel and service providers, including Google, as necessary to operate, host, and support the application. The application may process information alongside data from ReliaRide's configured transportation and fleet services to perform its dispatch functions. Google user data is not sold or used for advertising. Human access is limited to authorized operational use, support, security, and applicable legal or recordkeeping obligations.

ReliaRide X's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention, correction, and deletion

Workbooks, processing state, and audit records are retained as needed for transportation operations, troubleshooting, and applicable recordkeeping obligations. Contact scenicviewtransportation@gmail.com to request access, correction, or deletion of information. Requests are reviewed against access rights and operational or legal retention requirements; some historical audit records may need to be retained.

Reviewing or revoking authorization

You can review or revoke the application's access through your Google Account connections. Revoking access prevents further use of that authorization and may interrupt scheduled workbook processing. It does not automatically delete existing workbooks or previously retained records; contact us for a deletion request.

This public information site

These pages contain app information and this policy. They do not collect dispatch information, include a sign-in form, or use advertising or analytics scripts. The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process visitor information, such as IP addresses, for hosting and security under the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Updates and contact

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in application practices. The effective date above identifies the current version. For privacy questions or application support, contact scenicviewtransportation@gmail.com.
